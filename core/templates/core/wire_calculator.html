Wire Selection Guidelines
Residential

Typical household circuits use 1.5mm² (lighting), 2.5mm² (outlets), or 4mm² (high-demand circuits).

Industrial

Industrial applications often require 10mm², 16mm², 25mm² or larger depending on power demands.

Safety

Never use undersized wire. Always consult local electrical codes and have work done by qualified electricians.
