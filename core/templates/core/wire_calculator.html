Usage Profile Guidance
Short Duration
  • Typically less than 30 minutes at a time
  • Examples: kettle, toaster, blender, power tools
  • Can use a higher percentage of wire ampacity because the wire has time to cool
Normal Household Use
  • Typically 30 minutes to 3 hours
  • Examples: TV, desktop computer, washing machine, microwave
  • Balanced safety margin suitable for most residential loads
Continuous Load
  • Typically more than 3 hours continuously
  • Examples: air conditioners, servers, water pumps, lighting circuits, refrigerators
  • Requires derating because wires heat up over long periods

These time ranges are simplified guidance for homeowners and students. Actual electrical design should follow local electrical codes and manufacturer specifications.

How It Works
  1. Enter power in watts and voltage in volts, or enter current directly
  2. Click "Calculate Recommendations"
  3. System applies a 1.25× safety factor to the calculated current
  4. View all wire sizes that can safely handle the adjusted current
Wire Selection Guidelines
Residential

Typical household circuits use 1.5mm² (lighting), 2.5mm² (outlets), or 4mm² (high-demand circuits).

Industrial

Industrial applications often require 10mm², 16mm², 25mm² or larger depending on power demands.

Safety

Never use undersized wire. Always consult local electrical codes and have work done by qualified electricians.
