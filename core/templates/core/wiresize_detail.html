Safety Considerations

Do not exceed maximum ampacity: Using a wire smaller than recommended for the current flow can cause overheating, melting of insulation, and fire hazards.

Temperature derating: In high-temperature environments, the maximum safe ampacity may be reduced.

Voltage drop: Even if a wire can handle the current, it may still drop too much voltage for proper operation.

Always follow local codes: Consult your local electrical codes and regulations for specific requirements.
