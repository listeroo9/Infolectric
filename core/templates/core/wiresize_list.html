About Wire Sizes

Wire size is measured in square millimeters (mm²) and indicates the cross-sectional area of the conductor. Larger wire sizes can safely carry more electrical current.

Ampacity is the maximum amount of electric current a wire can continuously carry at a specified temperature rise without damaging the insulation or conductor.

Proper wire sizing is critical for safety. Undersized wires can overheat and cause fires, while oversized wires waste materials and money.
